## Regional bundle `{bundle}`
These questions are from a regional bundle: {rule}
Its players speak the language above and have a link to the region: family from there, lived or travelled there, follow its football, food or music. The section above still sets spelling, units, numbers and dates; what changes is what the players know.
- **Difficulty and distractors:** they live outside the region but know what such a family talks about at home; less than someone living there, more than the general market. Judge difficulty (scale 1–15) and distractors for them.
- **The region's own things keep their own names:** dishes, festivals, places, people, music, sayings ("arepa", "Feria de las Flores", "vallenato"; "Spätzle", "Schützenfest"). Add a short gloss in the target language only where the question can't be understood without it ("tinto (black coffee)"), never one that gives the answer away.
- **Regional words travel:** a question about a word or saying of the region (what a «tinto» is, what a „Schultüte“ is) is asked in the target language about the original word; these players know such words. `about_language` stays for questions on the spelling, grammar or wordplay of {source_language}.
- **`needs_local_knowledge` is not a reason here:** the whole bundle needs it. A question doesn't travel only for `wordplay`, `about_language` (as above) or `other`.
