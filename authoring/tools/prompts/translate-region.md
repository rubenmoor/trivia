## Regional bundle `{bundle}`
These questions are from a regional bundle: {rule}
Its players speak the language above and have a link to the region: family from there, lived or travelled there, follow its football, food or music. The section above still sets spelling, units, numbers and dates; what changes is what the players know.
- **Difficulty and distractors:** they live outside the region but know what such a family talks about at home; less than someone living there, more than the general market. Judge difficulty (scale 1–15) and distractors for them.
- **The region's own things keep their own names:** dishes, festivals, places, people, music, sayings ("arepa", "bandeja paisa", "Feria de las Flores", "vallenato"). Add a short gloss in the target language only where the question can't be understood without it ("tinto (black coffee)"), never one that gives the answer away.
- **Regional words travel:** a question about a regional Spanish word or saying (what a «tinto» is, what «parcero» means) is asked in the target language about the Spanish word; these players know such words. `about_spanish` stays for questions on Spanish spelling, grammar or wordplay.
- **`needs_local_knowledge` is not a reason here:** the whole bundle needs it. A question doesn't travel only for `wordplay`, `about_spanish` (as above) or `other`.
