# Task: more concepts

A subcategory of the trivia game described above is running out of unused concepts. You get its facets and every concept it already has. Add 40–80 new concepts.

- Use the existing facets, or add up to 3 new facets (short Spanish names, 2–5 words) for areas of the subcategory that the facets miss. Put new facet names in `new_facets`.
- A concept is a bare Spanish name: a thing, animal, plant, person, character, place, event, object, custom or term. **No facts, descriptions or question ideas.** Concrete and specific.
- Something a Colombian family with kids aged 6–16 could be asked about; nothing only an expert knows.
- **Not** a concept already in the list, nor a synonym, nickname, singular/plural or part of one.
- `familiarity` (1–5): how well kids aged 11–12 in a Colombian family know it. 5 = every kid knows it; 3 = kids have heard of it; 1 = only a keen 16-year-old knows it.
